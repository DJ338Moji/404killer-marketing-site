**404 Killer App Marketing Portal** is the high-converting commercial front-end, interactive ROI engine, agency partner hub, and automated storefront scanner for **404 Killer: Revenue Shield**.

---

## ⚡ Core Features & Capabilities

1. **Instant Free Storefront Scanner (`/audit`)**: Real-time link health scanner allowing prospective Shopify merchants and agencies to crawl store endpoints and quantify wasted ad budget.
2. **Interactive ROAS & ROI Calculator**: Dynamic slider calculating preserved revenue based on monthly store visitors, average order value (AOV), and baseline 404 bounce rates.
3. **Agency & Performance Partner Portal (`/agencies`)**: Showcases the 25% lifetime recurring rev-share, partner earnings calculator, hosted agency portal, and immutable client audit reports.
4. **Transparent 3-Tier Founder Pricing (`#pricing`)**:
   - **Starter Shield**: $8.95/mo or $60/yr ($5.00/mo effective)
   - **Pro Revenue Sentinel**: $14.95/mo or $125/yr ($10.41/mo effective)
   - **Agency & Brand Sentinel Portal**: $149/mo or $1,599/yr ($133.25/mo effective)
5. **Interactive Video Walkthrough Modal**: Step-by-step walkthrough explaining autonomous 404 auto-healing and ad spend protection.
6. **Master Customer Agreement & Privacy Policy (`/terms`, `/privacy`)**: Enterprise-grade SaaS legal agreements guaranteeing strict customer data non-disclosure and tamper-proof client audit reports.

## 🏗️ Architecture & Technology Stack

- **Framework**: Vite 7.3 + React 18
- **Styling**: TailwindCSS with CSS custom properties (`--card-bg`, `--card-border`)
- **Routing**: React Router DOM 6
- **Icons**: Heroicons + Lucide React
- **Animations**: Framer Motion
- **Hosting & CDN**: Vercel Edge Network (Auto-deploy on push to `main`)
- **Email Infrastructure**: Cloudflare Email Routing (`support@404killer.com`, `admin@404killer.com`)
